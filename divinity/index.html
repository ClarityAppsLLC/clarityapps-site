Divinity

The Bible App

A verse chosen for the day, guided reading plans, and somewhere to bring what you are carrying. Divinity is built for the few quiet minutes you actually have, not for the hour you don't.

A rhythm, rather than a reading list

Most people who want a daily habit with Scripture have started one before, somewhere in Genesis, and lost it by the second week. Divinity is built around returning rather than beginning.

  • A verse for the day, drawn from a pool of 365 so a year passes before anything repeats, with a morning reminder if you want one.
  • A reading streak and a calendar of the days you showed up, plus nine milestones along the way.
  • One life from church history each day, kept afterwards in a collection that fills as the year goes on.

The reader

Five translations, all of them public domain or openly licensed:

  • King James Version (KJV)
  • World English Bible (WEB)
  • WEB British Edition (WEBE)
  • Bible in Basic English (BBE)
  • Darby Translation (DRBY)

Bookmark a verse, highlight it, keep a note against it, mark a chapter read, and set the type as large as you need it. Tap any verse and Divinity will talk it through with you — plainly, the way a patient friend would, without pretending to settle what the Church has never settled.

Divinity is not a replacement for a pastor, a congregation or a counsellor, and it is written not to behave like one. On anything heavy it offers Scripture and comfort, and it points toward the people who are actually there.

The Prayer Wall

A place to ask other people to pray, and to pray for them. Post under your name or anonymously, and see how many people have prayed for what you wrote.

It is moderated: a language filter on every post, reporting and blocking on every card and every profile, and requests clear off the wall on their own rather than accumulating.

365 Christians who changed history

A martyr, a reformer, a nurse, a bishop, a teenager. Each one arrives with original stained-glass artwork made for this app, their dates, what they are remembered for, and a short account of the life. One a day, kept once it is yours.
