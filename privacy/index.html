Privacy Policy

Divinity: The Bible App  ·  Last updated: June 6, 2026

We built Divinity to help people grow in their faith. We collect only what's necessary to make the app work, we never sell your data, and we never will.

1. Who We Are

Divinity is an app created by Clarity Apps LLC. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at support@clarityapps.info.

2. What We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — if you create an account, we store your name and email address via Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Profile data — your display name and avatar preference, stored in Firestore.
  • Community content — prayer requests and study group messages you choose to post are stored in Firestore and visible to other users.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe, payment is handled by Apple's App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Usage preferences — settings like dark mode, offline mode, and widget preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and never transmitted to our servers.
  • Notification preferences — whether you've enabled daily verse reminders is stored locally on your device only.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • Your personal Bible reading notes and bookmarks are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To display your name in community features you opt into (prayer requests, study groups).
  • To verify your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and community data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — payment processing for iOS subscriptions. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — payment processing for Android subscriptions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers the Scripture Chat feature. Messages you send in chat are processed by Anthropic's API. Anthropic Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account), which removes your account data from Firebase. Community content you posted (prayer requests, group messages) may remain visible until manually removed.

7. Children's Privacy

Divinity is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, email us at support@clarityapps.info and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase's built-in security rules and encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact
