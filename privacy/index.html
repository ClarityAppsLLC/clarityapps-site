Privacy Policy

Divinity: The Bible App  ·  Last updated: October 15, 2026

We built Divinity to help people grow in their faith. We collect only what's necessary to make the app work, we never sell your data, and we never will.

1. Who We Are

Divinity: The Bible App is developed and operated by Clarity Apps LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), the data controller for personal information collected through this app. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at support@clarityapps.info.

2. What We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — if you create an account with your email, Apple or Google, Firebase Authentication (Google) stores your email address and your name. If you continue without signing in, Firebase gives the app an anonymous guest account instead, with no email address attached.
  • Profile data — your display name, the avatar icon you choose, and, if you add them, your favorite verse and the achievements you have earned. Your profile is stored in Firestore, and other Divinity users can see it, for example on the Prayer Wall and in Friends. If you use a photo from your library as your avatar, it stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Onboarding answers — during onboarding we ask your name, which Bible translation you want, when you would like your daily verse, where you would like to start reading, and what you would like the app to help with. Your name becomes your display name and is used in Scripture Chat (below). Your other answers are stored only on your device, though a personalized reading plan request uses some of them (below).
  • Reading data — if you have an account, your highlights, notes, bookmarks, chapters read, achievements, reading streak, saint card collection and reading plans are backed up to Firestore under your account, so they come back when you sign in on another device. If you use the app as a guest, they are stored only on your device.
  • Community content — prayer requests you post are stored in Firestore with your display name, avatar icon and account ID, and other users can see them. When you write a request, its text and your first name are sent through our server to Anthropic to write a short prayer, which is posted with it. A request is shown on the Prayer Wall for up to two days. After that it is no longer shown, but it stays stored until you delete your account. You can delete a request yourself while it is showing. We also store which accounts prayed for each request.
  • Friends and messages — if you use Friends, we store your friend code, your friends list, your friend requests and the direct messages you exchange, in Firestore. A conversation can be read only by the two people in it.
  • Reports — if you report a prayer request or another user, we store the report (what was reported, the reason, and the account IDs involved) so we can review it.
  • Scripture Chat — messages you send in Scripture Chat pass through our server (Google Cloud Functions) to Anthropic's API, which writes the reply. Each request also includes your first name and your chosen translation, so the reply can address you and quote the right Bible. Your chats are saved on your device, and if you have an account, your 10 most recent chats are also saved to your account so they sync between devices. Anthropic may retain messages for up to 30 days for trust and safety purposes, per its privacy policy.
  • Personalized reading plans — when you ask for a personalized plan, your answers to its questions (including what is weighing on you and anything you write in your own words), the books you have read in the app (and, for a follow-up plan, the chapters of the plan you just finished), the time of day you usually read, your translation and the testament you chose to start in are sent through our server to Anthropic to write the plan. Your name is not sent. The plan and your answers are saved on your device and, if you have an account, backed up to it.
  • Notifications — if you allow notifications, your device's push token is stored in Firestore so we can tell you when people pray for your request. Those notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google. Reminders such as your daily verse are scheduled on your device and do not use our servers.
  • App usage — we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used: for example, how far people get through onboarding, which sign-in option they choose, whether they allow notifications, when the subscription screen is shown and whether a purchase completes, and when the app runs into an error. Google Analytics also records your device model, operating system, app version and approximate location (such as your country), which Google works out from your connection. It does not receive your onboarding answers, messages, prayers or notes, it does not use your device's advertising ID, and we do not link it to your name or email. From version 1.1.0, the app does not send this data when your device's region is set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, Gibraltar or Switzerland, or when the app cannot read your device's region.
  • Device identifier — the app creates a random ID on your device. It is used for a few features before you have an account and as a fallback when checking your subscription. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe, payment is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage and verify subscription status. RevenueCat receives your account ID (a random ID, not your name or email), a device identifier, your app version and platform, and your purchase history (product ID and transaction timestamps). We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — crash reports and diagnostic information are collected by Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to help us identify and fix technical issues. This data includes device type, OS version, app version, and a stack trace of the crash. It does not include your personal content or message history. From version 1.1.0, crash reporting is also off in those cases, and any crash report kept on the device is deleted without being sent.
  • Feedback — if you send feedback or report a bug from inside the app, we store your message, the email address you give us (if any), your account ID, your platform and your app version.
  • Usage preferences — settings such as text size, Bible translation and reminder times are stored only on your device.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location or use your device's location services.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, use your device's advertising identifier, or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment card details — all payments are processed by Apple or Google.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To back up your reading data and restore it on your other devices.
  • To display your name, avatar and profile in community features you choose to use.
  • To run the Prayer Wall, Friends and direct messages, and to review reports.
  • To provide Scripture Chat responses, personalized reading plans and the prayers written for prayer requests, through Anthropic's API.
  • To send the notifications you have allowed.
  • To verify your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To respond to support requests and feedback you send us.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, through Google Analytics.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues through crash reporting.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating and improving the app.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions and App Check: signing you in, storing your backed-up data and community content, running our server code, and checking that requests come from the genuine app (using Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity). Data is encrypted at rest and in transit by Google. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — app usage analytics, as described in Section 2. Collection of the advertising ID and ad personalization are turned off. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting and diagnostics. Collects device type, OS version, app version, and crash stack traces. Does not collect personal content. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Receives your account ID, a device identifier, your app platform and version, and purchase history (product ID, transaction timestamps) to verify subscription status. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Expo (650 Industries) — delivers push notifications about your prayer requests. Receives your device's push token and the text of the notification. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for subscriptions. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers Scripture Chat, personalized reading plans and the prayers written for prayer requests. What Section 8 describes is sent over TLS-encrypted connections, through our server, to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic does not use API-submitted data to train its models. Chat data may be retained by Anthropic for up to 30 days for trust and safety review. Anthropic Privacy Policy

7. International Data Transfers

Clarity Apps LLC is based in the United States. If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with data transfer restrictions, your personal data will be transferred to and processed in the United States by our service providers (Google/Firebase, RevenueCat, Anthropic, Expo).

We rely on the following safeguards for these transfers:

  • Google (Firebase, Crashlytics) — transfers are covered by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted under EU Commission Decision 2021/914.
  • RevenueCat — transfers are covered by RevenueCat's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Anthropic — transfers are covered by Anthropic's data processing agreement and applicable SCCs.
  • Expo — Expo is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension.

You may request a copy of the applicable transfer safeguards by contacting us at support@clarityapps.info.

8. Automated Processing

Scripture Chat, personalized reading plans and the prayer written when you post a prayer request use Anthropic's Claude AI model. A chat request includes the messages in that chat, your first name and your chosen translation. A prayer request includes its text and your first name. A plan request includes what Section 2 describes under Personalized reading plans, without your name. Your other onboarding answers are never sent. This processing does not produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

9. Data Retention

Your account data, including the reading data backed up to it, is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account). That removes your Firebase Authentication record, your profile and backed-up data, your prayer requests, your friends, friend requests and direct messages, and your push tokens.

Prayer requests are shown on the Prayer Wall for up to two days and are kept until you delete your account. Reports and feedback are kept so we can act on them; deleting your account does not remove them, but we will delete them if you ask us at support@clarityapps.info. Google Analytics keeps event-level app usage data for no longer than 14 months. Crash diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat for the duration required by applicable tax and accounting law. Anthropic may retain Scripture Chat messages for up to 30 days per its policy.

Upon account deletion, we will request deletion of your data from RevenueCat within 30 days. We cannot guarantee deletion of data held by Anthropic beyond our API relationship; please contact Anthropic directly at anthropic.com/privacy for requests specific to their retention.

10. Children's Privacy

Divinity is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@clarityapps.info. We will respond within 45 days. For complex or numerous requests, we may extend this period by an additional 45 days and will notify you.

All users:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data. You can also delete your account directly in-app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account).
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g., community posts), you may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

EEA and UK residents (GDPR):

  • Data portability — receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV) and transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Restriction of processing — request that we restrict how we use your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Object to processing — object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.
  • Automated decision-making — you have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. As noted in Section 8, Scripture Chat does not produce such effects.
  • Lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (DPA). In the EU, find your DPA at edpb.europa.eu. In the UK, contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

13. Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between the app and our servers (Firebase, Anthropic, RevenueCat) uses TLS 1.2 or higher. No personal data is transmitted over unencrypted connections.
  • Encryption at rest — data stored in Firestore is encrypted at rest by Google using AES-256 by default.
  • Access controls — Firestore security rules limit each user to their own data, apart from shared actions such as praying for a request, and let users read only what is meant to be shared: prayer requests, other users' profiles, and the messages in their own conversations. No employee of Clarity Apps LLC has routine access to user personal data.
  • Faith data minimization — your onboarding answers stay on your device, except your name and the few a personalized plan request uses (Section 2). Anthropic receives only what Section 8 describes, and nothing is used for advertising.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and relevant regulatory authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.
