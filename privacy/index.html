Privacy Policy

Divinity: The Bible App  ·  Last updated: June 14, 2026

We built Divinity to help people grow in their faith. We collect only what's necessary to make the app work, we never sell your data, and we never will.

1. Who We Are

Divinity: The Bible App is developed and operated by Clarity Apps LLC. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at support@clarityapps.info.

2. What We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — if you create an account, we store your name and email address via Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Profile data — your display name and avatar, stored in Firestore and linked to your account. If you choose a custom avatar photo from your photo library, that image is uploaded and stored as part of your profile.
  • Faith journey information — during onboarding, we ask about your faith background and spiritual goals. This information is stored locally on your device and may be sent to our AI chat service (Anthropic) to personalize your Scripture Chat experience.
  • Community content — prayer requests you choose to post are stored in Firestore and visible to other users. Prayer requests are automatically deleted after 30 minutes.
  • Device identifier — an anonymous device ID is used to enable features for users who have not created an account, such as posting anonymous prayer requests. This ID is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe, payment is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — crash reports may be collected to help us identify and fix technical issues. This data does not include your personal content.
  • Usage preferences — settings like dark mode and font size are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • Your personal Bible reading notes, bookmarks, and highlights are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment card details — all payments are processed by Apple or Google.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To display your name and avatar in community features you opt into.
  • To personalize your Scripture Chat experience based on your stated faith goals.
  • To verify your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues through crash reporting.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and community data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for subscriptions.
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers the Scripture Chat feature. Messages you send in chat, along with your onboarding faith context, are processed by Anthropic's API. Anthropic Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account), which removes your account data from Firebase. Prayer requests are automatically deleted after 30 minutes. Other community content you posted may remain visible until manually removed or your account is deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

Divinity is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, email us at support@clarityapps.info and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase's built-in security rules and encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
