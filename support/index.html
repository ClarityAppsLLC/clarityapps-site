Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them from, not by us.

iPhone: open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and select Divinity.

Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, and select Divinity.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Make sure you're signed in to the same store account you bought it with — the same Apple Account on iPhone, or the same Google account on Android. A subscription bought on one platform can't be restored on the other.

Is my data backed up?

Bookmarks, reading progress, and notes are synced to your account and restored when you sign in on any device.

What Bible translations are available?

Divinity includes five English translations: the King James Version (KJV), the World English Bible (WEB), the WEB British Edition, the Bible in Basic English (BBE), and the Darby Translation. Switch between them in the app settings. The NIV, ESV, NLT, NASB, and NKJV are not available in Divinity.
